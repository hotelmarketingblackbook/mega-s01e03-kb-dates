owner Patryk Klesta accepted reviewed 2026-10-31 · 0 days ago

Ticket #4471 merged 412 incoming customer reports about dates landing on the wrong day. While merging by subject under Decision 0001 enabled rapid triage during morning incident peaks, detailed investigation demonstrates that two distinct engineering defects are conflated in #4471.

We have filed two separate issues on GitHub:

  • Issue #1 (379 reports): Shared calendar parser assumes month-first format (MM/DD) in European and day-first locales.
  • Issue #2 (33 reports): Booking-v3 form component displays a hardcoded MM/DD/YYYY placeholder hint to users in day-first locales.
Resolved conflation: Split #4471 into two isolated root causes with separate reproduction and repair paths.

The Two Defects

1. Parser Month-First Assumption (Issue #1 · 379 reports)

The backend parser reads all dates month-first (MM/DD). For European and day-first locales (da-DK, pl-PL, cs-CZ, nl-NL, de-DE, it-IT, pt-PT, es-ES, en-GB, en-IE, fr-FR), appointments on days 1–12 are inverted (e.g. 7 April becomes 4 July).

  • Volume: 379 reports (92.0% of queue)
  • Tenants (11): alder.physio, copper.studio, harbour.dental, kettle.books, lantern.row, long.table, morrow.tax, nine.lanterns, pale.harbour, ridge.clinic, stone.and.sons
  • Channels: App (204), Email (150), Form (25)
  • Time Window: 2026-04-01 to 2026-10-04
  • Desk Notes: 5 reports (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620) were noted as one-day shifts, but correctly belong under parser logic.

2. Booking-v3 Placeholder Hint Mismatch (Issue #2 · 33 reports)

In the booking-v3 web frontend, the date input field renders a hardcoded MM/DD/YYYY helper label. Users in day-first locales dutifully follow the prompt and type the month first. When submitted, the tenant's day-first configuration parses the month as the day, inverting the booking date.

  • Volume: 33 reports (8.0% of queue)
  • Tenants (3): alder.physio (de-DE), copper.studio (es-ES), pale.harbour (pt-PT)
  • Channels: 100% web Form (33)
  • Time Window: 2026-04-17 to 2026-09-22
